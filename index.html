About Me

I am Veronicah Gladys Namagembe, currently serving as the Managing Director of Pride Bank Limited, where I provide executive leadership and strategic oversight across a network of 44 branches and 3 contact offices across Uganda.


I have served at Pride Bank for 14 years, having previously served as Acting Deputy Managing Director for one year and Head of Operations for four years. Prior to joining Pride, I worked with Diamond Trust Bank Uganda for one year and Uganda Women’s Finance Trust, now Finance Trust Bank, for six years.


Throughout my career, I have built extensive experience in banking operations, strategic leadership, financial services, governance, risk management and institutional development. My leadership experience has also extended to industry and professional institutions, where I have contributed to strengthening the financial services sector and promoting sound institutional governance.


I previously served as National President of the Association of Microfinance Institutions in Uganda (AMFIU) and have held several board and leadership positions, including Board Member of Lubiri High School, Board Member and Chairperson of the Finance and Human Resource Committee of the Uganda Institute of Banking & Financial Services (UIBFS), and Council Member of UIBFS.


I currently serve as a Board Member of the Uganda Bankers Association, representing supervised financial institutions under Tier 2 and Tier 3, as well as Board Member and Vice Chairperson of Majestic Brands Ltd.


As Managing Director and Board Member of Pride Bank Limited, I participate in key Board Committees covering Risk, Assets & Liabilities; Compensation, Human Resource, Administration & Procurement; and Strategy & Compliance.


My professional focus is centred on strategic leadership, operational excellence, sound governance, effective risk oversight, financial inclusion and sustainable growth, with a strong commitment to strengthening institutions and creating lasting value for stakeholders.
