About Me

I am a Doctor of Leadership Candidate and a seasoned microfinance specialist with 21 years hands-on experience. I hold an MBA from Durham Business School, UK, as well as a Master of Science in Finance & Accounting and Bachelor of Business Administration from Makerere University Business School, Uganda.


At present, I hold the position of Managing Director at Pride Bank Ltd. Previous to that, I held the position of Acting Deputy Managing Director and Head of Operations, also at Pride.


I have also attended various Executive Leadership, Risk Management, Governance and Strategic Training courses from Harvard Kennedy School, Frankfurt School of Finance, Harvard Business School, London School of Economics, Boulder Microfinance Training and School of Applied Microfinance (Mombasa).


I am an Honorary Fellow with Uganda Institute of Banking & Financial Services. I have served as the National President of the Association of Microfinance Institutions in Uganda (AMFIU), a Board Member on Lubiri High School Board and as a Board Member of The Uganda Institute of Banking & Financial Services (UIBFS).


I am currently a Board Member of Uganda Bankers Association, representing all supervised financial institutions under Tier 2 & Tier 3.


Professionally, I sit on the Board Committees for: Risk, Assets & Liabilities; Compensation, Human Resource, Administration & Procurement; as well as Strategy and Compliance.
